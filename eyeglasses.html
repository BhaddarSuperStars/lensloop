Eydology's eyeglass range spans a $9.99 everyday tier up through premium acetate and metal frames near $30. Here's how the collections split, and how to pick one that fits.

01

Women's eyeglasses

The largest collection, organized loosely by shape: cat-eye, round, square, oval, rectangle, and oversized. Prices mostly sit between $9.99 and $22.95, with clear and tortoiseshell colorways the most consistently in stock.

  • Cat-eye and butterfly shapes suit rounder faces by adding angle.
  • Oval and rectangle frames tend to suit square or angular faces by softening the jawline.
  • If you're unsure, a mid-size rectangle or oval frame is the safest first pick.
02

Men's eyeglasses

Skews toward black, tortoise, and clear-grey acetate in square, rectangle, and browline shapes, generally $9.99–$25.99. The browline and aviator-adjacent styles run slightly larger, which suits broader faces.

  • Square and rectangle frames are the safest match for round or oval faces.
  • Browline styles (heavier on top) suit longer or narrower faces well.
03

Kids' eyeglasses

A dedicated kids' line in round and square shapes, typically $9.90–$15.90, sized smaller through the temple and bridge than the adult frames. Crystal and matte colorways are common, aimed at being sturdy rather than delicate.

  • Check the listed frame width against your child's current pair — kids' sizing isn't standardized across brands.
  • A spring hinge (flagged in the product description) holds up better to daily wear than a rigid hinge.
04

Picking a lens to go with it

The frame is only half the order — you'll choose a lens type at checkout. If you're not sure what you need, read our lens guide before you pick a frame, since some lens options (progressive, high-index) fit better in certain frame shapes than others.
